# Write a product pitch

Turn the owner's brief into a clear explanation of the app for an ordinary person considering it, not for agents managing a project. Adapt [the template](templates/PITCH.md) and save `PITCH.md` in the version's folder, `.project/projects/<project>/documents/development/<version>/`.

## The mission and the overview come first

The pitch explains what this version delivers toward the project its overview describes, within the mission, and what it leaves for later. Do not narrow, rename, or redefine the product to fit the version. If the brief conflicts with the mission or the overview, ask the owner. Never edit either from here.

## Content

- **What it solves:** what someone can accomplish, and the visible result.
- **How people use it:** a few short examples of an action, the app's response, and the result. No invented backstories.
- **What to learn from existing apps:** what a relevant app does and what this one adapts or simplifies, with a link to the source. A few focused fetches are enough; this is not a research report. When the owner supplies material, use it instead of browsing.
- **How it should look and work:** what is visible first, how main actions are reached, how states are told apart. When a visual reference exists, describe what it shows rather than inventing presentation.
- **This version:** what it delivers, what it leaves for later, and the owner's constraints.

Aim for 450 to 650 words in natural prose with few bullets. No status banners, metadata, tables, architecture, schedules, or lists of unknowns. Do not invent user circumstances, research findings, or numbers. Where a missing decision would make the pitch incoherent, ask the owner.

Check once for length and repetition, save, and return the path.
