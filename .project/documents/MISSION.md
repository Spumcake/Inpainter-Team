# Inpainter — mission

Inpainter is a creative production studio that makes professional-quality, human-directed visual content. Its marketing is built around results, for the clients it wants to attract: creative agencies, independent animation studios, and small viral brands.

Behind it is a human-directed, AI-native animation pipeline. Its key loop starts with a brief: the person describes what they want, and the agent turns it into a video. Unlike tools that go straight from the request to the output, Inpainter stops between them. The work moves through staged intermediates the person can see and change, so their intent is preserved before the final rendering pass.

Animation is where Inpainter starts, not where it ends. Its open-ended design invites extensions beyond that use.

Inpainter has two parts:

- **Inpainter Studio** is the software, and it is open source: desktop software where the person and the assistant shape the intermediates together. It works fully without the platform, with the person's own generation endpoints, such as OpenAI, fal.ai, or ComfyUI.
- **Inpainter Public** is everything public-facing that is not the software, and the commercial side of Inpainter:
  - **inpainter.app** is everything under that domain: the website, the dashboard, sign-in, the API, and the closed video and image generation platform behind them. The platform renders the intermediates, runs its own ComfyUI instance, and comes with skills that work only with it.
  - **Inpainter Docs** is the public documentation for both Inpainter Studio and the platform.
  - **media-team** is Inpainter's internal production studio. It makes Inpainter's own content, from trailers to original films, and tries tools first; those that would work well in the commercial product graduate into the platform.

Each project's overview says what it is in detail.
