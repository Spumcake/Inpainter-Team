## Attempts and result

Press R, O, or L and drag. The shape lands at the top of the sketch and keeps the tool's fill, stroke, dash, and corner radius. Fill starts off. Shift constrains that drag. Escape cancels it. Undo removes it. The eraser does not cut it.

The first window check saw no ink and a blank shape page. The debug binary was from before the shape sources. `cargo run` rebuilt it. The second check committed a rectangle, a square, a circle, and a 45-degree line.

Review found that a mixed fill was drawn as off and a mixed color as a gray well. Those controls now read as mixed.

The drag formula, the names, and the fill default were not written as formulas. The build used the readings in [plan.md](../plan.md). They are not owner decisions.
