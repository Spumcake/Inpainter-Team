## What we learned

The documents name a square, a circle, and 45-degree steps, and they do not give the formula. The build used one reading so a drag could be tried: the press stays put, the square uses the longer side, snap lands the press and the pointer on the grid before that constraint, and a drag that does not move adds nothing. Names follow Group N: Rectangle N, Ellipse N, and Line N. Constrain proportions and Snap to grid stay on the tool page. Releasing the drag does not select the new shape. Fill starts off, and turned on it is the light red from systems. Those are readings, not decisions the owner made.

A window check can pass or fail on the binary it launches. The first shapes check was a binary from earlier in the afternoon.
