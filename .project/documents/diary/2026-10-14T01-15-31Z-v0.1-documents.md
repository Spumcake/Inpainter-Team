## What we learned

The host-shell brief studied how Firefox handles a crashed tab. Firefox offers to send a crash report to Mozilla. The team noted that such a control needs a reporting service, which this stack does not have, and so it is not in v0.1.

The owner sees this as a useful feature for when Inpainter reaches production. Two parts would be needed:
- **Error handling and crash reports in the host.** When a sub-application crashes or reports an error, the host records what happened.
- **A service that collects those reports from users,** so failures across installations can be found and fixed.

A smaller first step needs no server: the host writes a local crash log that the person can open or attach to an issue. Neither belongs to v0.1, and neither is in the mission. They are proposals until the owner adds them.
